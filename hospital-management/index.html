🏛 Hospital Departments

Click on any department to see more details.

Cardiology

Deals with disorders of the heart and blood vessels. Equipped with advanced ECG, Echo, and Catheterization lab facilities.

Neurology

Specializes in disorders of the nervous system including the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. Offers EEG and MRI services.

Orthopedics

Focuses on the musculoskeletal system — bones, joints, ligaments, and muscles. Provides fracture care, joint replacement, and physiotherapy.

Pulmonology

Deals with diseases of the respiratory tract. Equipped with PFT lab and bronchoscopy facilities.

General Medicine

Covers diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of conditions in adults. First point of contact for most patients.

Emergency & Trauma

24/7 emergency care for accidents, injuries, and critical conditions. Fully equipped ICU and trauma center.
